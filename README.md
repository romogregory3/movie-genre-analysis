# 🎬 Movie Genre Success Analysis (2000–2024)

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes 24,000+ movies from Kaggle to identify high-revenue genres and assist film industry investors in making data-driven decisions.

## Key Insights
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** Science Fiction ($255M average revenue) and Adventure ($244M) lead all genres in worldwide box-office performance.
* **Blockbuster Effect:** High-performing franchises like *Avengers: Endgame* ($2.79B) heavily drive peak industry revenue.
* **Industry Trends:** 2020 saw a major revenue decline across all genres due to theater closures, followed by steady recovery in Sci-Fi and Action.

## Recommendations for Investors
1. **Focus on Top Genres:** Prioritize Sci-Fi, Adventure, and Action for high box-office upside.
2. **Combine Genres:** Blend high-performing elements (e.g., Sci-Fi + Action) to maximize audience reach.
3. **Prioritize Rewatchability:** Target concepts with long-term streaming and box-office durability.
